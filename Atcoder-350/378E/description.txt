You are given a sequence A = (A_1, A_2, \dots, A_N) of N non-negative integers, and a positive integer M . Find the following value: \[ \sum_{1 \leq l \leq r \leq N} \left( \left(\sum_{l \leq i \leq r} A_i\right) \mathbin{\mathrm{mod}} M \right). \] Here, X \mathbin{\mathrm{mod}} M denotes the remainder when the non-negative integer X is divided by M .

**Constraints:**
- 1 <= N, M <= 2 * 10^5
- 0 <= A_i < M
- All input values are integers
