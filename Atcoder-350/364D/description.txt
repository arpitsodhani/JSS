There are N+Q A_1,\dots,A_N,B_1,\dots,B_Q on a number line, where A_i has a coordinate a_i and B_j has a coordinate b_j . For each j=1,2,\dots,Q , answer the following question: Let X be the among A_1,A_2,\dots,A_N that is the k_j -th closest to B_j . Find the distance between X and B_j . More formally, let d_i be the distance between A_i and B_j . Sort (d_1,d_2,\dots,d_N) in ascending order to get the sequence (d_1',d_2',\dots,d_N') . Find d_{k_j}' .

**Constraints:**
- 1 <= N, Q <= 2 * 10^5
- -10^9 <= a_i <= 10^9
- -10^9 <= b_i <= 10^9
- 1 <= k_i <= N
- All input values are integers
