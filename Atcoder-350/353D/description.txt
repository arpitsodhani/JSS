For positive integers x and y , define f(x, y) as follows: Interpret the decimal representations of x and y as strings and concatenate them in this order to obtain a string z . The value of f(x, y) is the value of z when interpreted as a decimal integer. For example, f(3, 14) = 314 and f(100, 1) = 1001 . You are given a sequence of positive integers A = (A_1, \ldots, A_N) of length N . Find the value of the following expression modulo 998244353 : \displaystyle \sum_{i=1}^{N-1}\sum_{j=i+1}^N f(A_i,A_j) .

**Constraints:**
- 2\leq N\leq 2\times 10^5
- 1\leq A_i \leq 10^9
- integers
