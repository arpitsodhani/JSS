Takahashi, a patissier working at the ABC pastry shop, decided to sell assorted cakes to commemorate AtCoder Beginner Contest 400. The shop sells N kinds of cakes: cake 1 , cake 2 , \ldots , cake N . Each cake has three non-negative integer values: beauty, tastiness, and popularity. Specifically, cake i has beauty X_i , tastiness Y_i , and popularity Z_i . He considers pairing up these cakes into K pairs without overlaps. Formally, he will choose 2K distinct integers a_1,b_1,a_2,b_2,\ldots,a_K,b_K between 1 and N (inclusive), and pair cake a_i with cake b_i . The price of a pair formed by cakes a_i and b_i is \max(X_{a_i} + X_{b_i},\, Y_{a_i} + Y_{b_i},\, Z_{a_i} + Z_{b_i}) . Here, \max(P,Q,R) denotes the greatest value among P,Q,R . Find the maximum possible total price of the K pairs. You are given T test cases; solve each of them.

**Constraints:**
- 1 <= N <= 3000
- 1 <= M <= N(N-1)/2
- 1 <= a_i < b_i <= N
- All pairs (a_i, b_i) are distinct
- All input values are integers
