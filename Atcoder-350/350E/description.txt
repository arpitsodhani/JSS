You are given an integer N . You can perform the following two types of operations: Pay X yen to replace N with \displaystyle\left\lfloor\frac{N}{A}\right\rfloor . Pay Y yen to roll a die (dice) that shows an integer between 1 and 6 , inclusive, with equal probability. Let b be the outcome of the die, and replace N with \displaystyle\left\lfloor\frac{N}{b}\right\rfloor . Here, \lfloor s \rfloor denotes the greatest integer less than or equal to s . For example, \lfloor 3 \rfloor=3 and \lfloor 2.5 \rfloor=2 . Determine the minimum expected cost paid before N becomes 0 when optimally choosing operations. The outcome of the die in each operation is independent of other rolls, and the choice of operation can be made after observing the results of the previous operations.

**Constraints:**
- 1 \leq N \leq 10^{18}
- 2 \leq A \leq 6
- 1 \leq X,Y \leq 10^9
- integers
