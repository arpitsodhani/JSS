There are N people labeled 1 to N , who have played several one-on-one games without draws. Initially, each person started with 0 . In each game, the winner's increased by 1 and the loser's decreased by 1 (scores can become negative). Determine the final of person N if the final of person i\ (1\leq i\leq N-1) is A_i . It can be shown that the final of person N is uniquely determined regardless of the sequence of games.

**Constraints:**
- 2\leq N\leq 100
- -100\leq A_i\leq 100
- integers
