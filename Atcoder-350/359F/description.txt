You are given a sequence of integers A=(A_1,\ldots,A_N) . For a tree T with N vertices, define f(T) as follows: Let d_i be the degree of vertex i in T . Then, f(T)=\sum_{i=1}^N {d_i}^2 A_i . Find the minimum possible value of f(T) . The guarantee the answer to be less than 2^{63} .

**Constraints:**
- 2 <= N <= 10^5
- 1 <= A_i <= 10^9
- All input values are integers
