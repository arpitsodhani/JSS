There is one humidifier in the AtCoder company office. The current time is 0 , and the humidifier has no water inside. You will add water to this humidifier N times. The i -th addition of water ( 1 \leq i \leq N ) takes place at time T_i , and you add V_i liters of water. It is guaranteed that T_i < T_{i+1} for all 1 \leq i \leq N-1 . However, the humidifier has a leak, and as long as there is water inside, the amount of water decreases by 1 liter per unit time. Find the amount of water remaining in the humidifier immediately after you finish adding water at time T_N .

**Constraints:**
- 1 <= N <= 100
- 1 <= T_i <= 100
- T_i < T_{i+1}
- 0 <= V_i <= 100
- All input values are integers
