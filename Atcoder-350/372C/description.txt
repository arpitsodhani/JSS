You are given a string S of length N . You are also given Q queries, which you should process in order. The i -th query is as follows: Given an integer X_i and a character C_i , replace the X_i -th character of S with C_i . Then, print the number of times the string ABC appears as a substring in S . Here, a substring of S is a string obtained by deleting zero or more characters from the beginning and zero or more characters from the end of S . For example, ab is a substring of abc , but ac is not a substring of abc .

**Constraints:**
- 3 <= N <= 2 * 10^5
- 1 <= Q <= 2 * 10^5
- S is a string of length N consisting of 'A', 'B', and 'C'
- 1 <= X_i <= N
- C_i is 'A', 'B', or 'C'
