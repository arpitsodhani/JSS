You are given two strings S and T consisting of lowercase English letters. Determine if there exists a pair of integers c and w such that 1 \leq c \leq w < |S| and the following condition is satisfied. Here, |S| denotes the length of the string S . Note that w must be less than |S| . If S is split at every w characters from the beginning, the concatenation of the c -th characters of the substrings of length at least c in order equals T .

**Constraints:**
- S and T are strings consisting of lowercase English letters
- 1 <= |S| <= 100
- 1 <= |T| < |S|
