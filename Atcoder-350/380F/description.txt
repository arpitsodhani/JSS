Takahashi and Aoki will play a game using cards with numbers written on them. Initially, Takahashi has N cards with numbers A_1, \ldots, A_N in his hand, Aoki has M cards with numbers B_1, \ldots, B_M in his hand, and there are L cards with numbers C_1, \ldots, C_L on the table. Throughout the game, both Takahashi and Aoki know all the numbers on all the cards, including the opponent's hand. Starting with Takahashi, they take turns performing the following action: Choose one card from his hand and put it on the table. Then, if there is a card on the table with a number less than the number on the card he just played, he may take one such card from the table into his hand. The player who cannot make a move first loses, and the other player wins. Determine who wins if both players play optimally. It can be proved that the game always ends in a finite number of moves.

**Constraints:**
- 1 <= N, M, L <= 10
- 1 <= A_i, B_i, C_i <= 10^9
- All values in each sequence are distinct
- All input values are integers
