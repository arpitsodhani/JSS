In AtCoder Land, there are N popcorn stands numbered 1 to N . They have M different flavors of popcorn, labeled 1, 2, \dots, M , but not every stand sells all flavors of popcorn. Takahashi has obtained information about which flavors of popcorn are sold at each stand. This information is represented by N strings S_1, S_2, \dots, S_N of length M . If the j -th character of S_i is o , it means that stand i sells flavor j of popcorn. If it is x , it means that stand i does not sell flavor j . Each stand sells at least one flavor of popcorn, and each flavor of popcorn is sold at least at one stand. Takahashi wants to try all the flavors of popcorn but does not want to move around too much. Determine the minimum number of stands Takahashi needs to visit to buy all the flavors of popcorn.

**Constraints:**
- 1 <= N, M <= 10
- S_i consists of 'o' and 'x'
- |S_i| = M
