Takahashi is health-conscious and concerned about whether he is getting enough of M types of nutrients from his diet. For the i -th nutrient, his goal is to take at least A_i units per day. Today, he ate N foods, and from the i -th food, he took X_{i,j} units of nutrient j . Determine whether he has met the goal for all M types of nutrients.

**Constraints:**
- 1 <= N <= 100
- 1 <= M <= 20
- 1 <= A_i <= 10^6
- 1 <= X_{i,j} <= 10^6
- All input values are integers
